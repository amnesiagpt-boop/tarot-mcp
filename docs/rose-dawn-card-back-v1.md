# Рубашка «Розовая заря»

Создана 19 октября 2026 года для «Кода души» по запросу владельца встроенным генератором изображений Codex (без CLI). Новый авторский для проекта фон рубашки не заменяет лицевые изображения карт и их исходную атрибуцию.

Файл: `ui/src/assets/rose-dawn-card-back-v1.png`. Оригинальное разрешение: 1024 × 1536 пикселей, пропорции 2:3. Изображение сохранено без растягивания или искусственного увеличения. Один общий CSS ресурс используется во время настройки, перемешивания, снятия, выбора, переноса выбранных карт и до их раскрытия.

## Точный запрос генератору

Portrait 2:3 canvas, ideally 2048 x 3072 or higher. This is the actual flat printable card back image, NOT a product mockup. Full-bleed artwork fills every pixel of a rectangular canvas: no tabletop, hands, perspective, outer background, photographic card edges or cast shadow.

Visual language: elegant contemporary celestial luxury, romantic and luminous rather than dark occult. Rich dusty rose and berry-plum fields, warm blush pink (#f7e2e8), ivory (#fff9fb), ink plum (#472d3d), raspberry rose (#a54e72), soft rose-gold copper engraved linework. Add restrained opalescent rose, peach and lilac light inside ornamental details. Vibrant and richly colored while remaining refined and harmonious with a pale pink website.

Composition MUST be exactly two-way rotationally symmetric through 180 degrees, so there is no identifiable top or bottom during reversed tarot draws. A striking centered circular celestial rosette medallion with an elegant abstract eight-point star and two opposing intertwined crescent motifs, radiant engraved rays and concentric celestial geometry. Two matching mirrored floral arabesques with stylized rose blossoms, fine leaves, tiny stars and constellation-like dots toward the upper and lower ends. Refined double ornamental frame inset about 6 percent of the image, with all important ornament safely inset at least 8 percent so rounded card corners do not cut it. Symmetrical decorative corner filigree. Strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size, meticulous crisp print detail when enlarged. The center is luminous rosy ivory with subtle iridescent glow, edges are rich rose-plum with copper detail, avoid a black or muddy overall appearance. A jewel-like fine-art illustration with delicate engraved metallic ornament and exceptionally clean geometry. Sophisticated, uncluttered hierarchy.

No text whatsoever, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no faces or figures. No front-facing tarot card symbols that reveal a card's identity. No single directional sun face, no portrait. Render only the finished high-resolution artwork.
